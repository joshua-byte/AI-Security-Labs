IT POLICY
Document ID: D1
All university staff must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) when accessing university accounts. Staff must not share their account credentials with other people.

ATTENDANCE POLICY
Document ID: D2
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to meet the university's standard attendance requirement.

PASSWORD POLICY
Document ID: D3
University passwords must meet the institution's minimum length and complexity requirements. Passwords must not be shared with other people.

DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Document ID: D4
Confidential student information must be protected from unauthorized access. Access to student records is limited to authorized personnel.

INCIDENT REPORTING POLICY
Document ID: D5
Suspected information security incidents must be reported promptly to the university's IT security team. Reports should not expose passwords, authentication tokens, or other secrets.
